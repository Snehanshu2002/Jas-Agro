## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of ancient agronomic wisdom and deep computational biology. It marries the tactility and quiet permanence of living ecosystems with the razor precision of agronomic IoT, satellite remote sensing, and soil microbiome telemetry.

The aesthetic avoids the generic coldness of traditional enterprise SaaS and steers clear of folksy, rustic agricultural clichés. Instead, it balances high-end agrarian science, editorial luxury, and technical clarity. The atmosphere is quiet, authoritative, and organic: expansive warm ivory surfaces act as rich soil beds, anchored by monolithic deep-forest greens, obsidian typography, and sharp, bioluminescent spring lime telemetry accents.

Key characteristics:
- **Architectural & Asymmetric**: Layouts utilize broad fields of warm ivory broken by monumental vertical columns, offset cards, and sharp architectural divisions reminiscent of planned agroforestry plots and scientific journals.
- **Micro-Metric Precision**: High-density data readouts, micro-metric HUDs, sensor pings, and chemical soil composition tags contrast gracefully against expansive, breathable editorial narrative spaces.
- **Scientific Elegance**: Subtle micro-borders, organic noise textures over deep-tinted night-canopy fills, and refined pill-form indicators deliver a tactile, field-ready instrument aesthetic.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from living biological systems, rich humic soils, and high-precision sensor telemetry.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas & Field Foundations**: Warm ivory tones (`#FBFBF7` as the default surface background, `#F4F5EE` for sub-panels and subtle secondary elevations) provide an organic, non-glare resting plane that simulates premium unbleached technical paper.
- **Deep Canopy Primaries**: Deep Forest Green (`#163824`) and Nocturnal Canopy (`#0E2416`) command high authority and structural grounding. They serve as primary actions, inverted hero blocks, and structural dividers.
- **Living Botanical Midtones**: Rich Botanical Green (`#23623D`) acts as a transitional brand signifier for active biological states, vegetative health indices (NDVI), and verified ecological status.
- **Bioluminescent Telemetry Accents**: Luminous Spring Lime (`#A4DF39`) and Field Lime (`#72BF2B`) indicate real-time sensor updates, optimal thresholds, and actionable interactive micro-elements without falling into synthetic neon tropes.
- **Earth & Loam Tones**: Rich Terracotta (`#8B5E3C`) and Deep Humus (`#4D3826`) are reserved for soil-strata classifications, physical farm hardware telemetry, and warm tactile tags.
- **Typographic Obsidian**: Primary reading ink is Obsidian Slate (`#0E1811`), with secondary annotations set in Muted Canopy (`#1C2B20`) and field notes in Desaturated Forest Slate (`#4A5A4F`).

### Surface Application Rules
Never place pure white (`#FFFFFF`) against `#FBFBF7` without a soft tonal boundary or subtle structural border (`rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.08)`). Inverted surfaces utilize `#0E2416` with a low-opacity fine grain overlay and text tinted with `#F4F5EE`.

## Typography

Typography balances monumental editorial impact with scientific precision. Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean geometric underpinnings with open counters and balanced stroke weights that maintain high legibility in harsh field lighting as well as in dense boardroom dashboards.

### Editorial Hierarchy & Rhythm
- **Display Scales**: Colossal headlines feature aggressive negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.035em`) with tight line wrapping to mimic luxury agrarian broadsheets.
- **Editorial Statements**: Large paragraph intros use `body-xl` with deliberate, generous line height (`1.875rem`) to introduce agro-biological white papers and telemetry overviews.
- **Spec Labels & Micro-HUDs**: `label-metric` and `label-spec` are transformed to uppercase with deliberate positive tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.08em`). When rendering soil pH, nitrogen-phosphorus-potassium (NPK) ratios, and sensor telemetry, numbers must always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums`) to ensure strict columnar vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column dynamic grid designed to support dramatic asymmetric editorial blocks alongside data-heavy agronomic tables.

### Structural Framework
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid, 3rem page margins, 1.5rem gutters. Maximum container constraint of 1440px for editorial reading; wide viewports allow micro-metric HUD sidebars to lock to outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid, 2rem page margins, 1.25rem gutters. Cards reflow into staggered 4-column pairings.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, 1.25rem page margins, 1rem gutters. Horizontal micro-metric ribbons switch to smooth horizontal swiping tracks with snap-point alignment.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Layout rhythm uses modular pairs: generous structural padding (`space-xl`) between narrative chapters and dense grouping (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) inside IoT telemetry clusters.
- Asymmetric card spans: Key metrics dominate 7-column or 8-column blocks, paired with narrow 4-column or 5-column biological status panels to create editorial tension.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and biological rather than synthetic. The system relies on low-contrast structural ghost borders, tinted tonal layering, and fine organic field noise rather than generic drop shadows.

### Elevation Principles
- **Base Level (Canvas)**: Warm Ivory (`#FBFBF7`) acts as the continuous planar earth.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers)**: Panels sit on `#F4F5EE` bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.08)`. No blur or shadow is applied.
- **Tier 2 (Telemetry Overlays & Hover Cards)**: Soft tonal lift using a deeply diffused, vegetal-tinted shadow: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(14, 24, 17, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(14, 24, 17, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating HUDs & Glass Pills)**: Backdrop-filter frost (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with semi-translucent ivory (`rgba(251, 251, 247, 0.85)`) or semi-translucent night canopy (`rgba(14, 36, 22, 0.85)`). Edges are sharpened with an inner border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` on dark surfaces or `rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.12)` on light surfaces.
- **Textured Inverted Blocks**: Deep canopy blocks (`#0E2416`) employ an ultra-fine, monochromatic organic noise mask at 2.5% opacity to introduce paper-like soil depth.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses architectural rigor tempered by botanical softness. A default roundedness level of `1` (0.25rem / 4px base radius) maintains crisp, lab-grade structural borders for telemetry dashboards, data grids, and asymmetric cards.

### Shape Hierarchy
- **Base Containers & Data Panels (`rounded`)**: 0.25rem (4px). Clean, precise, and instrument-like.
- **Cards & Media Frames (`rounded-lg`)**: 0.5rem (8px). Provides subtle organic containment for satellite maps, spectral scans, and field reports.
- **Modal Dialogues & Hero Panels (`rounded-xl`)**: 0.75rem (12px). Reserved for primary layered overlays and high-impact editorial features.
- **Telemetry Tags, Status Micro-Badges & Interactive Pills**: Full continuous curve (`border-radius: 9999px`). The pill shape is exclusively reserved for floating status chips, sensor readouts, quick filters, and action buttons to distinguish interactive objects from structural layout blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid Nocturnal Forest background (`#0E2416`), ivory text (`#FBFBF7`), `0.25rem` radius or pill-shaped for quick inline actions. Subtle micro-scale interaction (`transform: translateY(-1px)`) with hover state deepening to `#163824` accented by a 1px border of `rgba(164, 223, 57, 0.3)`.
- **Secondary (Soil & Field)**: Unfilled with a 1.5px border of `#163824`, text in `#163824`. On hover, fills with `rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Bioluminescent Accent CTA**: Luminous Spring Lime (`#A4DF39`), high-contrast dark obsidian typography (`#0E1811`), reserved strictly for mission-critical actions, real-time trigger activations, and checkout flows.

### Chips & Sensor Tags
- **Micro-Metric HUD Chips**: Pill-shaped containers with a 1px border.
  - *Active Telemetry*: Background `#F4F5EE`, text `#163824`, containing an animated 6px pulse dot in `#72BF2B`.
  - *Biological Threat / Warning*: Background `rgba(139, 94, 60, 0.1)`, text `#8B5E3C`, border `rgba(139, 94, 60, 0.25)`.
  - *Tech-Spec Tag*: Font `label-spec`, uppercase, background `rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.05)`, letter spacing `0.08em`.

### Asymmetric Editorial Cards
- **Structure**: Asymmetric grid units featuring ivory backgrounds (`#F4F5EE` over `#FBFBF7`), separated by ultra-fine borders (`rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.08)`).
- **Header Structure**: Pairs a `label-spec` indicator (e.g., `ZONE 04 // MOISTURE FLUX`) with colossal inline data points set in tabular figures, followed by a succinct botanical description in `body-sm`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Flat ivory fills (`#FBFBF7`) with a 1px border in `rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.15)`, transitioning to an active border of `#163824` with an inner ring shadow of `0 0 0 1px #163824`. Placeholder text in `#4A5A4F`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 0.125rem radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Active state filled with `#163824` containing an crisp ivory tick; focus rings glow with a subtle spring lime ring (`0 0 0 2px rgba(164, 223, 57, 0.4)`).

### Lists & Spec Grids
- **Tabular Sensor Logs**: Border-bottom-only divider (`rgba(22, 56, 36, 0.08)`). Alternating row hover fills with `rgba(244, 245, 238, 0.8)`. Numerical data right-aligned in `label-metric`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Micro-Metric HUD**: Compact dark canopy modules (`#0E2416`) floating over satellite imagery or NDVI maps. Displays live soil conductivity, ambient canopy temperature, and sap flow index using lime and ivory typography.
- **Interactive Biological Strata Diagram**: Layered vertical cards transitioning smoothly from atmospheric sensors (top) down through leaf-canopy metrics, root-rhizosphere biological indicators, and deep loam hydration gauges.
